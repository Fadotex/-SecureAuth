# Student Guide

1. Register with your student ID, name, email, phone, accommodation block gender, and privacy consent. Verify the link sent to your email, then sign in.
2. Open **Find a room**, select a room type, and choose a specific room or **Assign me a room**. Full rooms are disabled. A successful selection holds a bed for 30 minutes.
3. Open **Payments**, choose the held booking, enter your MoMo phone, choose MTN or Telecel Cash, and enter at least the configured initial deposit. Approve the prompt on your phone. Payment status changes only after Paystack's signed webhook and server verification.
4. Use **My stay** for room, roommates, paid amount, balance, payment status, notices and receipts. Submit maintenance requests or a room-change request from **Notices & service**.
5. Sign out from the account menu. A booking hold expires if its required deposit is not verified before the deadline.
