# Staff / Secretary Guide

1. Sign in using the account created by the administrator. Set a private password at the first-login prompt, then complete mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) enrollment with an authenticator app when prompted.
2. Search resident records by student ID, name, or room. The staff view omits guardian contact information and revenue reports.
3. Record office cash or MoMo payments against a booking. Use a reference for office MoMo payments and give the resident their receipt details.
4. Check confirmed bookings in or check residents out. Use **Front desk** to log visitors and record departures.
5. Update maintenance work between Open, In progress, and Resolved. Staff cannot change room prices, manage staff accounts, access management reports, or edit audit history.
